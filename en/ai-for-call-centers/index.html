Where AI helps in a call center

Six tasks Czech-speaking voice AI can handle today. In the consultation we pick the ones that pay off for you.

Inbound lines without waiting

The AI answers every call, at peak times and after hours. It finds out what the caller needs, handles simple requests and passes the rest to an agent with a summary.

Lead qualification for agents

A short call checks interest, the right person and a good time. Your agents then call only the people who want to talk.

Callbacks on inquiries

Anyone who fills in a form or answers an ad gets a call within minutes, not the next day when interest has cooled.

Database cleaning

The AI recognizes numbers that no longer exist, phone menus, voicemail and people who want no more calls. Your data gets better with every campaign.

Every call result in your system

Result, name, callback time and reason go straight into your CRM or shared spreadsheet. Nobody retypes anything.

Call transcription and review

We transcribe and review your agents' calls: script adherence, objections, outcome. Coaching material without hours of listening.

What we learned in practice

Lessons from live campaigns that we build into every new deployment.

Warm contacts work best

AI is strongest where the call is expected or the brand is known: inbound lines, callbacks and existing customers. We will recommend where to start accordingly.

Callbacks need their own script

People call back the number the AI called them from. For those calls we set up a separate assistant that knows why we called.

Data decides the result

Dead numbers, switchboards and phone menus drag down every campaign. We show them to you right after the first batch.

Compliance by design

When you call thousands of people, the law matters as much as the technology. We plan for it from the first draft.

The AI always says it is AI

In the first sentence the caller hears that they are talking to an AI and that the call is recorded. Asked directly, the AI never denies it. Article 50 of the AI Act requires this.

Consent for automated calls

Under Czech law, a marketing call made by an automated system needs the recipient's prior consent, businesses included. Before every campaign we review where your contacts come from.

Data processing agreement

We sign a data processing agreement before the first call. Results are written into your systems.

We respect "do not call"

Anyone who wants no more calls is logged and never called again. You get the list too.

Voicelix does not provide legal services within the meaning of the Czech Legal Profession Act. We design solutions in line with the law. For a binding legal opinion we will recommend a lawyer.

FAQ

Didn't find your answer? Call Klára at +420 910 926 750 or book a consultation with Pavel.

Can the AI call customers on its own?

Yes, but under Czech law a marketing call made by an automated system needs the recipient's prior consent, businesses included. The easiest cases are inbound lines and callbacks to people who asked for them. We review where your contacts come from before every campaign. This is not legal advice.

Does the caller know they are talking to AI?

Yes. In the first sentence the AI introduces itself as an artificial intelligence assistant, says the call is recorded and never denies it when asked. Article 50 of the AI Act requires this.

How many calls can the AI handle at once?

Calls run in parallel. We set the pace to match how many interested contacts your people can take over. In the pilot for CIRCLE Company a.s. the AI called 118 contacts in 35 minutes.

Can you connect the AI to our system?

We write results into Google Sheets or into your CRM through its API. We check your specific system during the consultation.

What if the person calls back?

The call is taken by a separate callback assistant. It knows why we called and logs the result the same way as an outbound call.

How much does AI for a call center cost?

We quote individually based on call volume, integrations and scope. We usually start with a fixed-price pilot, after which you know where AI pays off.
