3. AI chat on the website

The website chat is run by an AI assistant, not a person. It always confirms this when asked directly. To generate replies, we send your messages to the Claude language model by Anthropic.

  • We do not store the conversation ourselves. During your visit it is kept only in your browser (sessionStorage) and disappears when you close the tab.
  • We store your contact and the conversation transcript only if you send us your contact yourself and agree that we get back to you.
  • According to its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use API data to train models. It may retain it for a limited time for safety and abuse prevention.
  • Please do not enter sensitive data in the chat (health data, national ID numbers, card numbers, passwords).

4. AI phone assistants

At +420 910 926 750 you will be answered by Voicelix AI assistants (Klára, Tomáš, Jana). They introduce themselves as AI at the start of the call. We process the call as follows: speech is converted to text, a language model prepares a reply, and the reply is converted back to voice. For quality control we keep the transcript and possibly an audio recording of the call for 90 days.

If you book a meeting through the assistant, it is entered in Pavel Šrámek's calendar and we send you a confirmation.

5. Business outreach

We send personalized offer emails to companies our services could help. The outreach is prepared by an AI agent and checked by a person before sending. We take the data from public sources: company websites, business profiles on Google Maps and public registers. We contact business contacts only.

You can object to outreach at any time by replying to our email or writing to pavel@voicelix.ai. We will then not contact you again and will keep your data only to the extent needed to make sure we don't contact you again.

Some processors are based outside the European Economic Area. We transfer data to them on the basis of a European Commission adequacy decision (for the USA, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where the recipient is certified), otherwise on the basis of standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. We will provide a copy of these safeguards on request.

8. Cookies and browser storage

The website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, which is why we do not ask for your consent. For the chat to work, we store the conversation in your browser's sessionStorage, only while the tab is open.

The website font is loaded from Google Fonts, so your IP address reaches Google. When you open the booking calendar, a Google Calendar page loads and Google may use its own cookies according to its policies.

9. Your rights

You have the right:

  • to access your data and receive a copy,
  • to have inaccurate data corrected,
  • to erasure, if we no longer need the data or process it unlawfully,
  • to restriction of processing,
  • to portability of data you provided to us on the basis of a contract,
  • to object to processing based on legitimate interest, including business outreach,
  • to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, uoou.gov.cz.
Send your request to pavel@voicelix.ai. We will reply without undue delay, within one month at the latest. To make sure we don't release data to someone else, we may ask you to verify your identity.
