Frequently asked questions

Can Klára connect to our booking system?

Today Klára books into Google Calendar. Connecting to another booking system is assessed case by case, depending on the interface your system offers.

Does the assistant give medical advice?

No. Klára is set up not to give medical advice. For urgent problems she refers the patient to the doctor or to the 155 emergency line.

Is an AI receptionist in a practice GDPR compliant?

We build GDPR in from the first design: the assistant works only with the data it needs, a data processing agreement is part of the engagement, and recording is set up the way you want. It is a solution designed in line with the law, not a legal service.

Do patients know they're talking to AI?

Yes. Klára introduces herself as an AI assistant at the start of the call and never denies it when asked. Art. 50 of the EU AI Act requires this.
